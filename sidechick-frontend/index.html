Chat. Play. Poof.

Sidechick is a free web app for adults (18+). Meet someone new in a random chat, play compatibility and trivia games with a partner or a stranger, or open a private room for two. No sign-up. Nothing is saved.

  • Random chat with optional interests, skip any time
  • Eight games: Same Brain, Hot Takes, Know Me, Compatibility Quiz, Couple Trivia, Flirt Mode, Truth or Lie, Spicy or Sweet
  • Same-phone mode: pass the phone back and forth
  • Private rooms for exactly two people, with view-once photos
